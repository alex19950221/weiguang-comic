2. What the app does

LumiComic is a local file reader. It does not provide, sell or recommend any comic content, offers no content search, sources or plug-ins, and never uploads your files anywhere.

3. Your content and copyright

Import only files you have the right to use. You are responsible for the content you import. The app does not handle files protected by digital rights management (DRM) and offers no way around such protection; it tells you when a file is encrypted or protected and cannot be opened.

4. Free features and Pro

Importing and reading every supported format, Single, Spread and Scroll reading modes, series organisation, groups, reading positions and bookmarks are free forever, with no timed trial. Features that are free at public launch will never become paid or limited. LumiComic Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks auto-crop margins, App Lock and hidden series, Read in place, batch organising, auto page turning and image filters; Pro features added later are included at no extra cost. Pro supports Family Sharing and Restore Purchases; refunds are handled by Apple under its rules.

5. If you lose Pro

After a refund, or on a device without the purchase, your library, reading positions, bookmarks and settings keep working; only new Pro actions stop. An existing App Lock can still be unlocked and turned off, but new locks cannot be added.

6. Files and data

The app never writes to your original files. By default it keeps a copy inside the app when you import; with Pro's Read in place it stores only the file access permission provided by the system. Deleting a comic from the library removes the in-app copy, reading position and bookmarks, never the original. Data stays only on your device; version 1.0 has no backup and the developer cannot recover it for you, so please keep your originals.

7. Formats and compatibility

Version 1.0 supports ZIP, CBZ, PDF and image-based comic EPUB; RAR/CBR and importing image folders directly are not supported yet. Encrypted or password-protected files, DRM-protected files and mostly-text EPUBs cannot be imported, and damaged pages may not display fully. Actual support depends on your installed version.
