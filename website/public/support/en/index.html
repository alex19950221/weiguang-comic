1. How do I import comics?

Tap Import comics in the library and pick one or more files in Files. Version 1.0 supports ZIP, CBZ, PDF and image-based comic EPUB. Importing keeps a copy inside the app and never changes your original files; duplicates are skipped automatically. With Pro you can use Read in place to open originals in Files without copying them. RAR/CBR, importing image folders directly, and importing via Share or Open in from other apps are not supported yet; you can zip the images into a ZIP or CBZ first.

2. Series, volumes or chapters are in the wrong order

The app reads series, volumes and chapters from file names and sorts them in natural order, for example Vol.3 or Ch.12. If the order is still wrong, send us a screenshot of the file names; there is no need to send the comics.

3. Reading modes, direction and zoom

Tap the middle of the page to show the controls. In Reader settings you can choose Single, Spread or Scroll mode, and left-to-right or right-to-left; in spreads you can show the cover alone or pair it with page 2. When a comic file carries a manga direction flag, the book opens right-to-left automatically. Double-tap or pinch to zoom, and use Contents and thumbnails to jump between pages.

4. File names look garbled

The app detects archive file names encoded in GBK, Big5 and Shift_JIS. If names still look garbled, tell us which tool and system created the archive and include a screenshot.

5. A file will not open

Encrypted or password-protected archives and PDFs, DRM-protected files and mostly-text EPUBs are not supported, and the app tells you when it meets one. If another file will not open at all, tell us its format and size.

6. The app takes up too much space

Imported comics are kept as a copy inside the app. Deleting a comic from the library removes the in-app copy, reading position and bookmarks, never your original file. Downsized page images live in the system cache and may be cleared by the system when space is low. Pro's Read in place does not copy originals.

7. New device or reinstalling

Version 1.0 does not offer backup or restore. Your library, reading positions and bookmarks are stored only on this device and are removed when you delete the app; on a new device or after reinstalling, import your comics again. Please keep your original files.

8. Pro, Restore Purchases and refunds

LumiComic Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks auto-crop margins, App Lock and hidden series, Read in place, batch organising, auto page turning and image filters. Find it in Settings → LumiComic Pro; on a new device or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchase there. Pro supports Family Sharing. To request a refund, use Apple's refund page; the developer cannot process payments directly.

9. Contact us

Email support@surveykit.cc with your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened, ideally with a screenshot. Please do not attach copyrighted comic files. We will reply as soon as we can.
