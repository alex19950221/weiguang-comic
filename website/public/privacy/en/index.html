1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the LumiComic (微光漫卷) iOS app, the Android app and this website. The iOS parts match Settings → Privacy Policy in the app; see “Android app” for how the Android version differs. LumiComic is an offline image reader that only opens your own files. The developer is liaozhiliang; contact support@surveykit.cc.

2. What we do not collect

There are no accounts or sign-in. The app contains no ads, analytics or third-party SDKs, and it does not track you.

The app itself makes no network requests. Your comics, library, reading progress and settings never leave this device.

3. Data stored on your device

Imported comics: by default the app keeps a copy inside the app and never modifies the original file. Copies are excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

Read in place: only the file access permission provided by the system is stored; the original is not copied.

Library information: series and groups, reading progress, bookmarks, reader settings and import history are kept in the app’s local database.

Page cache: downsized images generated for faster page turns are kept in the system cache folder and may be cleared by the system when space is low.

This data is used only by the app on this device. You can delete comics in the app; deleting the app removes all of the data above.

4. System features

File access: the app can only read files you choose in the system file picker.

Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode (app lock): verification is handled by iOS. The app only learns whether it succeeded and cannot access your biometric data.

In-app purchase: purchases and restores are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app only reads from the system whether Pro is unlocked; the developer never receives your payment details or Apple Account information.

Crash reports: if you choose to share with app developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports that do not identify you. Sharing is up to you.

5. When you contact us

If you email us, your email address, description, screenshots or files are used only to reply and resolve your issue, never for marketing, and are deleted once no longer needed. Please do not send copyrighted comic files or personal details unrelated to the issue; a screenshot of the file names is usually enough.

6. Support website

This website is static, with no analytics scripts, cookies, forms or third-party fonts. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may process access logs such as IP addresses and browser details for security and operations.

7. Android app

The Android app likewise has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not request internet permission or send network requests.

It reads only files you select in the system file picker and saves a copy in its private directory. It does not request all-files access or scan your device. If you select a cloud file, its document provider may need to download it; the app itself does not connect to the internet.

Library information and the page cache are handled as described above. Android system backup is disabled; clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local library and imported copies, so keep your original files.

The current Android app does not include purchases, app locking or biometric authentication.

8. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the update will ship with an app update and the effective date above will change.

If you have questions, email support@surveykit.cc or use the “App Support” link on the App Store product page.

Questions about this page? Contact support@surveykit.cc.
